Help and policies

How Meadowbrook Clinic's online services work. Times are clinic time; fees are in pounds.

Finding a clinician

  • Search finds a clinician by any part of their name, such as a surname.
  • Choosing a specialty lists everyone who offers it, whatever else they do.
  • A clinician's profile shows the days and hours they work. You can only book them on those days.
  • "Next available" is the earliest time you could book with them right now.
  • Clinicians on leave can't be booked until they're back.

Booking

  • Book online from tomorrow up to 28 days ahead. For a same-day appointment, call us at 8am.
  • We're open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00, and closed for lunch from 12:30 to 13:30. We're closed at weekends and on the closed days shown on the booking page (bank holidays and training days).
  • A standard appointment is 15 minutes. A long appointment is 30 minutes and has to finish by the end of the clinician's session: by 12:30 before lunch, or by the time they finish for the day.
  • You can't have two appointments at the same time.
  • You can book for a child on your Family page. The appointment is in the child's name.

Your appointments

  • Appointments lists your upcoming appointments and your children's, soonest first.
  • The count on your Home page is your own appointments that are still booked.
  • Cancelling is free up to the day before the appointment. Cancelling on the day costs £15. There's never a fee for a child under 16.
  • When you cancel, the time goes straight back on offer to other patients.
  • You can move an appointment to any free time with the same clinician. It keeps its length.
  • "Add to calendar" downloads the appointment with its date and time.

Repeat prescriptions

  • You can ask for up to 56 tablets or capsules (two months), or 2 inhalers, in one request.
  • One request per medicine every 28 days. Each medicine shows the day you can next ask for it.
  • If a medicine's review is overdue, book a medication review first: we can't issue it until then.
  • Prescriptions are ready to collect 2 working days after we get your request. Weekends and closed days don't count.

Test results

  • A value is in range when it's between the lower and upper limits, including the limits themselves.
  • The results list shows how many values in each test are outside their range, whether high or low.
  • "Since last test" shows whether a value went up or down compared with the previous test.
  • Your results are private: only you and your clinicians can see them. Reception staff can't.

Messages

  • Messages need a topic, a subject and some text (up to 1,000 characters). We reply within 2 working days.
  • Opening a reply marks it as read.
  • Messages aren't for emergencies: call 999, or 111 when we're closed.

Family

  • Add your children under 16. Anyone 16 or over registers for their own account.

Your details

  • An NHS number has 10 digits; we check the last one, which is a check digit.
  • Your date of birth can't be in the future.
  • Phone numbers are UK numbers with 11 digits, such as 07700 900123.

At reception

  • Reception sees appointments and contact details, never test results.
  • Checking in marks that one appointment as arrived.
  • Times reception keeps free (for emergencies, say) can't be booked online.
  • The day list download has only the appointments that are still on, not cancelled ones.
